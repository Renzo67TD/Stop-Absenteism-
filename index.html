    Why Present Po!?

    At roll call, your name is called and you answer, "Present Po!" It takes a second to say, yet it carries a big message: I'm here, and I matter.

    That is the heart of this campaign. A learner who is here is seen, counted and welcome. A learner who is away is missed. Present Po! is our way of making sure everyone knows it, whether they are in their seat today or on their way back.

    Why this campaign

    Three simple ideas guide everything we do.

    Small days, big picture

    Each day in class is one more piece of what you are learning. Miss a few and the picture gets harder to follow.

    There is always a reason

    Sickness, worry, a hard trip to school or a tough time at home can keep someone away. We start by listening, not blaming.

    Better together

    School feels like home when learners, parents and teachers look out for one another.
