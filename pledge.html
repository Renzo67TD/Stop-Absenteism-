Take the Pledge

Words become action when you put your name to them. Print, sign and share your pledge.

How it works

Pick the way that suits you. Every way ends with your class adviser, so your pledge is recorded.

Print it

Print this page (Ctrl+P on a computer, or Share → Print on a phone). Read each promise and make sure you mean it, then sign and date your card.

Edit it in Word

Prefer typing? Download the Word card, fill in your details, then print and sign it.

Pin it online

Use the form on the Pledge Wall below to pin your pledge and email it to the school.

However you pledge, hand your signed card to your class adviser to be recorded and added to the Pledge Wall.

My Present Po! Pledge

Ilocos Norte National High School

I pledge to…

  • Do my best to come to school every day I am able.
  • Arrive on time and ready to learn.
  • Tell a trusted adult when something makes school hard for me.
  • Catch up on what I miss and ask for help when I need it.
  • Check in on classmates who are absent.
  • Include others and never tease or judge anyone for missing school.
  • Help make our school a place where everyone belongs.

For class advisers: keeping a record

A simple routine so every pledge is counted and nothing gets lost.

Collect

Gather signed pledge cards from your class, on paper or printed from the Word file. Pledges sent by email can be printed or saved.

Record

Tick each learner off on the class roster as their pledge arrives. The roster is a Word file, so you can add rows.

Download the class roster ↓

File

Keep the roster with the signed cards as your class record, and share it with the campaign team or guidance office.
